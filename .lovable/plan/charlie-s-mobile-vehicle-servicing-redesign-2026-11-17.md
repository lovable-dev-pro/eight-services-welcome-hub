# Charlie’s Mobile Vehicle Servicing redesign

## Goal
Create a polished, client-ready three-page website prototype that feels true to Charlie’s existing black, white and electric-blue brand while making mobile servicing and quote requests easy to understand.

## Pages and experience
- **Home:** Distinctive visual introduction, existing logo and tagline, mobile-service positioning, featured maintenance and repair options, and clear routes to services and contact.
- **Services:** All eight supplied services, concise descriptions, supplied service intervals and included-work lists, plus quote links.
- **Contact:** Supplied phone, email and WhatsApp links, with a validated quote enquiry form.
- Keep the supplied business details accurate; do not invent prices, coverage areas, reviews or opening hours. Use a custom automotive service photograph because no additional photos were supplied.

## Technical approach
Use three separate pages with shared navigation and footer, semantic design tokens and page-specific titles/descriptions/social metadata. Preserve the user’s uploaded logo as an app asset. Store valid enquiries with Lovable Cloud so they have a real destination; owner email delivery remains off until a verified sending domain is available.

## Validation
Check navigation and quote links, form validation and the available submission path, the routing test, and the desktop and mobile layouts in the live preview.
